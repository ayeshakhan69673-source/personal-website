About me

I'm a full-stack developer and a Computer Science graduate of the University of Gujrat (class of 2026). I build complete web applications with Node.js, Express and MySQL, and I'm comfortable with every step: designing the database, writing the server code and secure login, building the pages people see, and putting the site online.

My biggest project is QalamAid. It is a live scholarship platform that connects verified students with donors. I built the system with protected passwords, expiring verification links, a practice Easypaisa payment system and an AI chatbot powered by Google Gemini. It runs on Vercel with a TiDB Cloud database.

I also built a school website with registration and login backed by a database, and I create business and online store websites with WordPress, Elementor and WooCommerce. I'm also building mobile apps with React Native and Expo, including Committee Tracker, an app for managing savings committees.

I earned merit-based scholarships in three semesters of my degree, and I'm now looking for a full-stack developer role where I can keep growing.
